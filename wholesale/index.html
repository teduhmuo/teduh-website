What We Offer

Six ways we work with other businesses

Coffee beans
Wholesale roasts for cafés and corporate pantries, sourced and roasted in-house — order from as little as 5kg. Free delivery within the Muar radius, with shipping available outside it. Our standard is a single-origin Brazil medium-dark espresso roast, built to suit a wide range of coffee drinkers.
Coffee consulting
Menu development, sourcing, and setup guidance for new cafés getting started. We also train your baristas and walk through the costing of coffee-based drinks, plus machine positioning, calibration, and maintenance.
Machine rental
Espresso machine and grinder rental, bundled with a bean supply plan. Full commercial setup from RM500/month, with a 1-year contract — bundle in your coffee bean supply and get free training and free maintenance included.
Pizza dough & shells
Frozen dough balls and par-baked Neapolitan shells, ready for your kitchen. Shells just need a reheat in a home microwave or oven — or start from frozen dough if you want the full pizzeria experience with your own technique and equipment.
B2B support
Ongoing support to help scale your business, from supply planning to operations. We advise on what you and your customers actually need, so you can deliver the service with confidence.
Coffee catering
We'll make sure your event runs smoothly, without you having to think twice about how to delight your guests. Rule of thumb: a free cup of coffee brightens someone's day like a morning sun.
