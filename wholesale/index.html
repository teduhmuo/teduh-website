Coffee Beans

Wholesale roasts for your cafe or pantry

We roast in-house and supply from as little as 5kg per order, so you're not locked into a large minimum just to get started. Our standard offering is a single-origin Brazil medium-dark espresso roast — a reliable, crowd-pleasing profile built to suit a wide range of coffee drinkers, from casual pantry drinkers to café regulars.

Delivery is free within the Muar radius, and we can arrange shipping if you're further out. Pricing scales with volume — reach out and we'll work out a rate that fits your order size.

Coffee Consulting

Everything a new café needs to get started right

Opening a café involves more decisions than just picking a bean. We help with menu development, sourcing, and general setup guidance — the kind of groundwork that's easy to get wrong the first time around.

We also train your baristas directly, walk through how to cost your coffee-based drinks properly, and help with machine positioning, calibration, and ongoing maintenance — so your setup runs smoothly from day one.

Ask about Coffee Consulting
Machine Rental

A full commercial setup without the upfront cost

We rent out an espresso machine and grinder, bundled with a bean supply plan — a full commercial setup starting from RM500/month, on a 1-year contract. Bundling in your coffee bean supply with us also gets you free training and free maintenance included for the length of the contract.

This is a good fit if you want to test running a coffee counter without committing to buying equipment outright.

Ask about Machine Rental
Pizza Dough & Shells

Neapolitan pizza, ready for your kitchen

We supply frozen dough balls and par-baked Neapolitan shells. The shells are the simpler route — just reheat in a home microwave or oven, no pizza-making technique required. If you want the full pizzeria experience, start from frozen dough instead and bring your own technique and equipment to the bake.

Either way, it's a way to serve genuine Neapolitan-style pizza without building a pizza kitchen from scratch.
