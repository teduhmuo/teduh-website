Our Story

Small shop, bigger plans

Teduh started small — a modest coffee shop built on very little capital, with a much bigger dream behind it. Over five years, we've grown from serving the customers who walk through our door to supplying coffee beans and beverages to other businesses, specializing in coffee products for both commercial and home use. Along the way, we brought Neapolitan pizza into our kitchen — using an authentic technique to bring out an authentic taste. That pizza is now something we serve dine-in, and something we help other businesses and home cooks bake at their own place, through our frozen dough and pre-baked shells. And through it all, we haven't forgotten where we started — we still make room for arts and culture, supporting events on our own premises and elsewhere. Today, we're expanding further still, building our own online presence to bring Teduh beyond these four walls.
